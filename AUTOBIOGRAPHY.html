About Me

Hello! My name is Joeylee Rose T. Tacgos, but you can call me Joeylee for short. I am 18 years old and I live in Masiwa, Marigondon, Lapu-Lapu City. I am the eldest daughter of our family. I love adorable things and cats.I am a person who've always believed that learning is a lifelong journey and that every new experience is an opportunity to grow. This belief drives my eagerness to learn new things and embrace challenges with an open mind. Whether it's picking up a new hobby or diving into a new subject, I am always excited to expand my horizons.

  • Reading: I love reading books on fantasy and science fiction. Stories that transport me to different worlds and times captivate my imagination.

  • Watching series: I love watching series, especially K-dramas and anime. It's great way to relax, and I enjoy thriller, action and fantasy genres.
  • Music: I enjoy listening to Christian songs, pop, and acoustic music. Some of my favorite artists include Taylor Swift, Laufey, and Niki.
